# ExpressJobs Manual Job Posting Offer

`PAYMENTS_LIVE=OFF`

## Offer

Human-assisted job publication for people who need workers quickly but do not want to learn a platform yet.

## Packages

| Package | Price | Includes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Basic | $500 UYU | One manual task post, clean wording, category, zone, WhatsApp contact path. |
| Intermediate | $1000 UYU | Basic plus improved description, 1 revision, and repost reminder. |
| Urgent | $1500 UYU | Intermediate plus priority manual follow-up and highlighted wording. |

## Delivery

- Human collects job details manually.
- Human creates the post or prepares content for staging/preview review.
- No payment is processed in the app.
- No sensitive data is required.

## Required Intake

- Task type.
- Zone.
- Date/time.
- Budget range if available.
- Contact method.
- Safety notes.

## Boundaries

No guaranteed worker, no employment promise, no legal/labor guarantee.
