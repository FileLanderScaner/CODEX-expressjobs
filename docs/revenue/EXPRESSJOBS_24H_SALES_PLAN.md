# ExpressJobs 24 Hour Sales Plan

`REAL_USERS_CONTACTED_BY_CODEX=false`

## Goal

Get one paid manual sale or one confirmed deposit within 24 hours.

## Hour 0-1

- Select 30 leads.
- Prioritize businesses with poor/no landing page.
- Prepare WhatsApp Business profile and payment method privately.

## Hour 1-4

- Send 10 landing page messages.
- Send 5 banner founder messages.
- Send 5 manual job posting messages.

## Hour 4-8

- Follow up every reply within 15 minutes.
- Push one clear package.
- Ask for manual confirmation.

## Hour 8-12

- Deliver first drafts for paid/committed leads.
- Send second batch of 10 messages.

## Hour 12-24

- Follow up warm leads.
- Record outcomes.
- Prepare tomorrow's list.

## Target

- 30 messages.
- 6 replies.
- 3 qualified leads.
- 1 paid manual sale or deposit.
