# ExpressJobs Sales Team Roles

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Roles

| Role | Owner | Tasks | Daily Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Operator | Human | Send WhatsApp Business messages manually, qualify leads, ask for deposit/payment outside the app. | Conversations, offers sent, closed payments. |
| Delivery Operator | Human/Codex-assisted | Prepare landing copy, banner draft, manual job post, and delivery checklist. | Delivered assets and pending revisions. |
| Revenue Tracker | Human/Codex-assisted | Update manual tracker without bank data or sensitive personal data. | Daily revenue dashboard. |
| Safety Reviewer | Human/Codex-assisted | Confirm no production promise, no in-app payment, no sensitive data in repo. | GO/NO-GO for each delivery. |

## Daily Routine

1. Select 20 leads.
2. Send 10 landing messages first.
3. Send 5 banner messages.
4. Send 5 manual job posting messages.
5. Follow up yesterday's warm leads.
6. Record only non-sensitive status.
7. Deliver paid assets.

## Definition Of Success

- At least 20 manual messages per day.
- At least 5 replies per day.
- At least 2 qualified offers per day.
- At least 1 paid manual sale as soon as possible.
- Zero production/payment/security violations.
