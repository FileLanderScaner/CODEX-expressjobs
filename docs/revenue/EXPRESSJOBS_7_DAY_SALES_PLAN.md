# ExpressJobs 7 Day Sales Plan

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Day 1

Sell landing pages manually. Goal: 30 messages, 1 sale.

## Day 2

Follow up all Day 1 leads. Publish Facebook/Instagram posts manually. Goal: 2 sales or deposits.

## Day 3

Push founder banners to shops and service providers. Goal: 1 banner sale.

## Day 4

Create sample landing screenshots/drafts for warm leads. Goal: improve close rate.

## Day 5

Open Workana/Fiverr profiles and send platform-compliant proposals manually. Goal: 5 proposals.

## Day 6

Package testimonials or examples from delivered work. Goal: proof asset.

## Day 7

Review revenue, leads, objections, and conversion. Decide what to double down on.

## Weekly Targets

- 150 manual messages/proposals.
- 25 replies.
- 10 qualified leads.
- 3-5 paid manual sales.
