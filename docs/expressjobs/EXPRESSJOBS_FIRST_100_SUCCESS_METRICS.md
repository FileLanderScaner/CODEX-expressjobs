# ExpressJobs First 100 Success Metrics

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Required Events

- `signup_started`
- `signup_completed`
- `role_selected`
- `job_created`
- `job_viewed`
- `job_application_created`
- `job_application_accepted`
- `message_sent`
- `job_completed`
- `review_created`
- `whatsapp_share_clicked`

## First 10 Success Criteria

- 10 testers complete onboarding.
- At least 3 jobs are published.
- At least 5 applications are created.
- At least 1 client-worker flow is simulated or controlled.
- Feedback from at least 5 users.
- 0 critical trust/safety incidents.

## First 25 Success Criteria

- 20+ onboarding completions.
- 8+ jobs created.
- 15+ applications.
- 3+ accepted applications.
- 10+ feedback responses.

## First 50 Success Criteria

- At least 40 activated users.
- Client-worker ratio remains usable.
- 10+ jobs created.
- 25+ applications.
- 5+ message threads.
- No unresolved critical safety incidents.

## First 100 Success Criteria

- Clear top categories.
- Repeat users appear.
- WhatsApp share drives at least 10% of testers.
- Trust objections are understood.
- Release gate remains honest: no production without staging validation.
