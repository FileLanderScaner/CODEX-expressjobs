# ExpressJobs Pricing Review From AI Studio

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Prototype Pricing

The AI Studio prototype included a "Premium Pro" card at `$9.99/mes` with unlimited applications, verified badge, reduced commission, early access, and priority support.

## Decision

Adapt the pricing structure but not the live price or claims.

## Safe MVP Pricing

- Free: limited publish/search, basic application, basic profile.
- Premium worker: profile highlight, more applications, early alerts, lower future commission, priority support.
- Commission: suggested 15% on completed jobs once payments/legal/disputes/provider are ready.

## Rejected For Now

- Presenting `$9.99/mes` as a real charge.
- "Verified Pro" badge before verification exists.
- "Priority support" as a live promise before support process exists.
- Reduced commission as a contract before payment terms are approved.

## UI Update

The `/pricing` route now presents pricing as proposal/staging information and states that real payments are inactive.
