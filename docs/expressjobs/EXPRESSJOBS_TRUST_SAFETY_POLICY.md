# ExpressJobs Trust Safety Policy

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Public Brand

User-facing brand: `Trabajos Rapidos`

Technical project: `ExpressJobs`

## Core Rules

- Do not promise guaranteed employment.
- Do not promise guaranteed income.
- Do not claim active payments.
- Do not claim identity verification until verification exists.
- Do not claim protected payments until provider, escrow/dispute, and legal processes exist.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
- Keep pilot jobs low-risk and observable.

## User Guidance

- Coordinate inside the platform while testing.
- Avoid sharing sensitive personal data.
- Use simple, legal, safe tasks for pilots.
- Report unclear, unsafe, or suspicious flows to the pilot operator.

## Admin Guidance

- Admin MVP is read-only.
- No destructive admin actions are available.
- Critical incidents block cohort expansion.
