# ExpressJobs Feedback Form

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Form Fields

1. Name or tester code.
2. Role tested: client, worker, admin observer.
3. Did you understand what ExpressJobs does?
4. What task would you publish or apply for?
5. What was confusing?
6. What made you trust it?
7. What made you distrust it?
8. Did you think payments were active?
9. Did anything imply guaranteed work or income?
10. Rating from 1 to 5 for clarity.
11. Rating from 1 to 5 for trust.
12. Would you test it again?
13. Would you recommend it to someone?
