# ExpressJobs First 100 Feedback Plan

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Collection Methods

- Short interview after test.
- Feedback form.
- Observer notes.
- Funnel event review after staging tracking exists.

## Interview Questions

1. What did you think ExpressJobs does?
2. Which role did you choose and why?
3. Would you publish a job or apply to one?
4. What made you trust or distrust the flow?
5. Did anything feel unsafe or unclear?
6. What information was missing?
7. Would you recommend this to someone in your neighborhood?

## Triage

| Severity | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Safety, privacy, payment confusion, or trust incident | Stop expansion and fix. |
| High | Blocks job creation or application | Fix before next cohort. |
| Medium | Causes confusion but tester can continue | Add to UX backlog. |
| Low | Copy or polish issue | Batch for weekly iteration. |
