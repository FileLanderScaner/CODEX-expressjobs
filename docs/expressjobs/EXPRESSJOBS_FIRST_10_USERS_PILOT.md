# ExpressJobs First 10 Users Pilot

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Pilot Objective

Validate whether 10 known testers understand ExpressJobs and can complete the main flows without production systems.

## Tester Mix

- 3 clients in Montevideo.
- 3 workers/changueros/oficios.
- 2 students looking for extra income opportunities.
- 1 small shop.
- 1 neighborhood entrepreneur.

## Pilot Script

1. Explain that this is a controlled MVP test, not a live employment marketplace.
2. Ask client testers to describe a real task they might publish.
3. Ask worker testers to identify jobs they would apply for.
4. Observe onboarding and role selection.
5. Run one controlled publish/apply/accept/message/review flow.
6. Ask for feedback using the feedback form.

## Pass Criteria

- 10 onboarding completions.
- 3 job posts.
- 5 applications.
- 1 controlled end-to-end flow.
- 5 feedback responses.
- 0 critical safety incidents.

## Stop Criteria

- Users believe income is guaranteed.
- Users think payment is already active.
- Users do not understand who they are contacting.
- Users try to share sensitive personal data.
- Main routes fail during testing.
