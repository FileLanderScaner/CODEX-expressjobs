# ExpressJobs Job Content Rules

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Allowed Pilot Jobs

- Small moving help.
- Simple cleaning.
- Basic repairs that do not require certified work.
- Local delivery of permitted items.
- Product photos for small businesses.
- Event setup support.
- Garden or patio help.

## Not Allowed In Pilot

- Illegal goods or services.
- Emergency, medical, security, or high-risk work.
- Electrical/gas/structural work requiring certification.
- Adult, exploitative, discriminatory, or unsafe tasks.
- Jobs asking users to share sensitive documents in chat.
- Payment requests outside the controlled pilot.

## Moderation Action

Unsafe content should pause the test flow and be recorded in the feedback tracker with severity `critical` or `high`.
