# ExpressJobs First 10 Tester Selection Criteria

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Status

External testers are `NO-GO_UNTIL_PREVIEW_AND_RLS_PASS`.

## Required Profile Mix

| Segment | Count | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potential clients | 3 | Validate job publishing, trust copy, and task clarity. |
| Workers/changueros/oficios | 3 | Validate job discovery, application, and profile expectations. |
| Small shops | 2 | Validate local business use cases and operational language. |
| General observers | 2 | Validate broad comprehension and navigation. |

## Location

Prefer Montevideo or the first intended local operating zone. If using other LATAM locations, mark them as out-of-market observers.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Adult tester.
- Understands this is a controlled MVP test.
- Can join a 20 to 30 minute guided session.
- Agrees to anonymous feedback collection.
- Uses WhatsApp or a browser regularly.
- Fits at least one target segment.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Minor.
- Expects guaranteed employment.
- Expects guaranteed income.
- Needs emergency work or urgent paid help.
- Wants to process real payments during the session.
- Is uncomfortable with a non-production MVP test.

## Tester Codes

Use anonymous tester codes:

- `T10-C01` to `T10-C03` for clients.
- `T10-W01` to `T10-W03` for workers.
- `T10-B01` to `T10-B02` for small businesses.
- `T10-O01` to `T10-O02` for observers.

Do not commit names, phone numbers, emails, or private notes.
