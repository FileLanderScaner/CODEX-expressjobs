# ExpressJobs Product Spec

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

ExpressJobs helps people in Uruguay/LATAM hire nearby workers for local tasks.

## Roles

- Client: publishes jobs, reviews applications, accepts a worker, chats, completes and reviews.
- Worker: creates a profile, sees open jobs, applies, coordinates, completes work, builds reputation.
- Admin: audits abuse, categories, disputes, and safety workflows.

## MVP Workflows

1. User lands on the site.
2. User signs up and selects client or worker role.
3. Client creates a draft/open job.
4. Worker views open jobs and applies.
5. Client accepts a worker.
6. Participants coordinate with basic messages.
7. Job moves through tracking states.
8. Completed job can receive reviews.
9. Jobs can be shared through WhatsApp.
10. Monetization CTAs explain commission and premium options without live charges.
