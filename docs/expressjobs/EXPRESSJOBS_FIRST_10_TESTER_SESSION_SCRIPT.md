# ExpressJobs First 10 Tester Session Script

`PRODUCTION_STATUS=NO-GO_PRODUCTION`

## Session Status

`READY_DRY_RUN_ONLY`

Do not run with external testers until Preview, Supabase staging, real RLS smoke, browser smoke, and release gate pass.

## Opening

Say:

> Esto es una prueba controlada de Trabajos Rapidos. No es produccion, no hay pagos reales activos, no se garantiza empleo y no se prometen ingresos. Queremos ver si se entiende la propuesta y donde aparecen dudas.

## Pre-Session Checklist

- Confirm tester code.
- Confirm tester segment.
- Confirm no minor is participating.
- Confirm session environment: Preview only after gate pass.
- Confirm no real payment will be tested.
- Confirm feedback will be anonymized.

## Moderator Rules

- Do not explain too much before the first interaction.
- Ask the tester to think aloud.
- Record confusion without correcting immediately.
- Note any trust, safety, payment, privacy, or employment misconception.
- Stop the session if the tester tries to enter sensitive personal data.

## Core Questions

1. What do you think this app is for?
2. What would you click first?
3. Is it clear which role fits you?
4. Is it clear whether payments are active?
5. Is it clear whether jobs or income are guaranteed?
6. What makes you trust or distrust the product?
7. What would stop you from using it?

## Close

Thank the tester, confirm no real job or payment was created, and ask them to complete the feedback form.
